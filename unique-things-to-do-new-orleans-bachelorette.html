Unique Things to Do for a New Orleans Bachelorette

Unique New Orleans bachelorette party ideas beyond Bourbon Street: live music, vintage shopping, architecture, streetcars, local cocktails, great food and neighborhood wandering.

START WITH WHERE TO STAY

Make the city the theme

New Orleans already has a stronger personality than any manufactured party theme. Build the weekend around brass bands, balconies, Creole architecture, cocktails, vintage finds, oysters, beignets and the neighborhoods themselves.

Go where the music is

Frenchmen Street is an easy starting point for live music, while Tipitina’s is worth checking when the calendar lines up. The point is to hear New Orleans, not just use it as a backdrop.

Shop for something you will wear again

Spend an afternoon on Magazine Street or hunt vintage at local shops. A piece found in New Orleans makes a better souvenir than disposable party décor.

Choose one beautiful ritual

A streetcar ride, jazz brunch, courtyard cocktail, long dinner, balcony morning or architecture walk gives the weekend texture. Pick the ritual that suits the group and leave the rest for the next trip.
