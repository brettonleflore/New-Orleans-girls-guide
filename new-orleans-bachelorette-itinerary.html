A 3-Day New Orleans Bachelorette Itinerary

A unique three-day New Orleans bachelorette itinerary with restaurants, cocktails, Frenchmen Street music, shopping, brunch and neighborhood time—without turning the weekend into a checklist.

START WITH WHERE TO STAY

Friday — arrival with a point of view

Check in, unpack, put on something better than airport clothes and start with cocktails. Follow with a destination dinner and live music on Frenchmen Street. Keep the first night close enough to home that the group still likes each other Saturday morning.

Saturday — the main event

Brunch, then spend the afternoon shopping Magazine Street or hunting vintage. Return to get ready together, book the dinner everyone will remember, then choose cocktails, dancing or a second music stop. One excellent plan per part of the day beats six rushed reservations.

Sunday — recover beautifully

Choose jazz brunch or a long lunch, add beignets and coffee, then take a streetcar or wander a neighborhood before departure. Sunday should feel like an epilogue, not another endurance event.

The rule that makes the itinerary better

Do not schedule every hour. New Orleans rewards detours: a band you hear through an open door, a courtyard you did not know existed, a second round you did not plan and a shop that becomes everyone’s favorite stop.
