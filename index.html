Sleep Well.

Luxury hotels set the standard. For a girls trip, the right private home can change the entire weekend.

OUR PICK FOR GROUPS

Maison Saint LaFleur

The private-house experience with the polish of a boutique stay.

Beautiful shared spaces, a chef's kitchen and unmistakable New Orleans character make the stay part of the trip—not simply where everyone sleeps. For groups who actually want to be together, Maison Saint LaFleur is the home base we'd start with.

THE MODERN SPLURGE

Four Seasons New Orleans ↗

Five-star service, a gorgeous pool and polished contemporary luxury on the Mississippi. The benchmark when the group wants the full resort-level hotel experience.

CLASSIC FIVE-STAR

The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans ↗

Grand and traditional, with a spa, courtyard and full-service hotel amenities for groups who prefer individual rooms and classic luxury.

OLD-WORLD NEW ORLEANS

The Roosevelt New Orleans ↗

A glamorous historic property with one of the city's great hotel lobbies and the legendary Sazerac Bar. It's a little removed from the heart of the French Quarter, so for late nights we prefer a rideshare over a longer walk.

RESERVATIONS WORTH MAKING

Tables We Love.

Not a generic best-of list. These are the rooms that make sense for a girls trip: beautiful, delicious, celebratory, or impossible to forget.

SEAFOOD + CHAMPAGNE

The Crustacean Club ↗

Oysters, caviar, fries and Champagne—or commit to the tasting menu. Tiny, polished and exactly the right kind of indulgent.

THE EXPERIENCE

Mosquito Supper Club ↗

A love letter to bayou cooking served like a dinner party. Book it when the meal itself is the evening’s headline.

CHEF-DRIVEN

Compère Lapin ↗

Nina Compton’s Caribbean point of view meets Louisiana ingredients. Chic without trying too hard, with cocktails that deserve their own stop.

INTIMATE TASTING

Saint-Germain ↗

Small, serious and quietly cool. The reservation for the food girls who would rather have ten beautiful courses than a scene.

THE DRESS-UP DINNER

Restaurant R'evolution ↗

Grand-room energy, a deep cellar and modern Creole cooking. Wear the thing you packed because you needed an excuse.

PRETTY + PARISIAN

MaMou ↗

Romantic brasserie energy on Rampart—florals, French technique, very good wine and a room that photographs beautifully.

OLD QUARTER ROMANCE

Irene's ↗

Warm, intimate and old-school in the best way. Italian-Creole comfort with the feeling that someone local let you in on it.

THE PINK ICON

Brennan's ↗

Breakfast, Champagne, courtyard photos and Bananas Foster. It is iconic for a reason—and yes, the pink facade understands the assignment.

LUNCH + JAZZ BRUNCH

Commander’s Palace ↗

Go for the grand New Orleans ritual: dressed-up lunch, famously inexpensive martinis, or jazz brunch when the weekend calls for a little ceremony.

UPTOWN CLASSIC

Clancy's ↗

White tablecloths without the tourist script. Uptown, polished and very New Orleans—the kind of reservation that feels like knowing someone.

COCKTAIL-LED DINNER

Jewel of the South ↗

A jewel-box cottage with serious food and even more serious cocktails. Start here when you want dinner to slide elegantly into drinks.

DESTINATION DINNER

Dakar NOLA ↗

Senegalese tasting-menu storytelling through a New Orleans lens. Thoughtful, transportive and worth building the evening around.

MAGAZINE STREET

La Petite Grocery ↗

Understated Uptown sophistication. The move when the group wants a beautiful dinner without needing the room to shout about it.

FINE DINING

Restaurant August ↗

Chandeliers, tasting-menu energy and the full white-tablecloth fantasy. Save it for the night everyone agrees to go all in.

THE TASTING MENU

Emeril's ↗

New Orleans culinary history, reworked as a modern tasting experience. A serious reservation for a group that plans trips around dinner.

STEAK NIGHT

Doris Metropolitan ↗

Dry-aged steak, dark-room glamour and a little edge. Order for the table and let dinner run late.

GULF SEAFOOD

GW Fins ↗

For the night when everyone wants immaculate Gulf seafood and a dining room that still feels like an occasion.

DINNER WITH PERSONALITY

Jack Rose ↗

Color, art, Champagne and a dining room that knows how to have fun. Better for dinner than brunch—and very good for photos before dessert.

SUSHI

Nagomi ↗

Chef Kaz’s intimate sushi experience. Tiny, precise and ideal when the group wants something completely different from another Creole dinner.

WHEN THE NIGHT GOES LONG

Verti Marte ↗

Not chic. Essential. When it is very late and everyone is a little tipsy, order the All That Jazz po-boy and understand why locals keep sending people here.
