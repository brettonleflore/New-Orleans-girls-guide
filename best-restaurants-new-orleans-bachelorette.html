Best Restaurants for a New Orleans Bachelorette Party

A curated guide to the best New Orleans restaurants for bachelorette parties and girls trips, from celebratory dining rooms and tasting menus to brunch and late-night classics.

START WITH WHERE TO STAY

Dinner should feel like an event

For the big night, choose a room with energy and food worth remembering. Our shortlist includes Compère Lapin, MaMou, Restaurant R’evolution, Emeril’s, August, Dakar NOLA, Saint John, Acamaya, Irene’s and Doris Metropolitan.

For the group that cares about food

A bachelorette dinner does not have to mean sacrificing the meal for the scene. New Orleans is one of the easiest cities to build a celebration around serious cooking. Reserve early, tell the restaurant your group size accurately and choose the place that matches the bride rather than the loudest trend.

Brunch without wasting the day

Brennan’s and Commander’s Palace deliver classic New Orleans occasion energy; Bearcat is a more casual reset. The best brunch is the one that fits the rest of the day rather than consuming it.

Late-night insurance

Verti Marte belongs in the plan precisely because not every great New Orleans food memory requires a reservation, a dress code or a white tablecloth.
