Best New Orleans Cocktail Bars for a Girls Trip

The best cocktail bars in New Orleans for a bachelorette weekend or girls trip: polished classics, romantic hotel bars, neighborhood favorites and places worth dressing up for.

START WITH WHERE TO STAY

Start with the classics, then wander

Sazerac House gives the group context; Jewel of the South and Bar Tonique reward people who actually care what is in the glass. Lafitte’s Blacksmith Shop supplies history and atmosphere in a completely different register.

For a dressed-up drink

The Columns, The Chloe, Hot Tin and Jolie work when cocktails are part of the outfit. They are useful anchors before dinner or as the first stop of a longer night.

Do not make Bourbon Street the bar guide

Bourbon is an experience, not a complete cocktail strategy. Give it a cameo. Build the rest of the night around bars and neighborhoods with their own point of view.

Group rule

For a larger party, check reservation policies and group limits before arriving. A bar that is perfect for four may not be the right first stop for twelve.
