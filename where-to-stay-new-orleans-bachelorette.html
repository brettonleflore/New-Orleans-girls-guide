Where to Stay in New Orleans for a Bachelorette Party

Where should a bachelorette group stay in New Orleans? Compare Esplanade Avenue, the French Quarter, CBD, Garden District and Marigny for walkability, nightlife, group atmosphere and style.

START WITH WHERE TO STAY

Choose the neighborhood before the hotel

The right New Orleans location depends on the trip. French Quarter puts the action outside the door. Esplanade Avenue gives you historic architecture between the Quarter and Frenchmen. The CBD offers polished hotels. Uptown and the Garden District trade late-night proximity for leafy streets, restaurants and Magazine Street.

Our pick: Esplanade Avenue

Esplanade is a strong fit for a girls trip that wants access without sleeping in the middle of the loudest blocks. Maison St. LaFleur is a historic Greek Revival residence with Parisian-inspired interiors, a private balcony and the kind of shared atmosphere that makes a group stay feel like part of the trip.

French Quarter vs. a house stay

A French Quarter hotel is convenient for first-time visitors who want maximum immediacy. A character-filled house can be better when the group values gathering space, getting ready together and having a beautiful base between plans. Decide whether your group prioritizes lobby service or private shared space.

What to look for when booking

Prioritize the number of real bedrooms, bathrooms, common space, nighttime transportation, luggage logistics and the distance to the places you will actually visit. A beautiful address is only useful if it works for the group.
